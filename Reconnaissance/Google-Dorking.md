\# Google Dorking – Passive Reconnaissance



\## Objective

To understand how advanced Google search operators help identify publicly indexed information.



\## Tool

Google Search



\## Search Operators Used

\- site: – Restricts results to a particular website.

\- "phrase" – Searches for an exact phrase.

\- filetype: – Searches for a particular file type.

\- -keyword – Excludes results containing a specified keyword.



\## Observations

Record the search queries used and the types of public results returned.



\## Conclusion

Google Dorking helps security researchers discover publicly indexed information through advanced search operators. It should be performed ethically and without attempting unauthorized access.
