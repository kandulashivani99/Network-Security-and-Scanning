\# Shodan – Passive Reconnaissance



\## Objective

To understand how Shodan identifies publicly accessible internet-connected devices and their exposed services.



\## Tool

Shodan



\## Search Queries

\- apache

\- port:80



\## Information Observed

\- Identified services

\- Exposed port information

\- Country information, where available

\- Organization or ISP information, where available



\## Conclusion

Shodan helps security researchers understand the public exposure of internet-connected systems through indexed service information. Searches were performed for educational and passive reconnaissance purposes only.
